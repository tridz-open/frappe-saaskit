# Payments

`saas_core` takes payment through a **Gateway Account**. Razorpay in `order` checkout mode is the verified gateway; Stripe and Razorpay subscription mode are experimental.

<Callout type="warning">Use **test-mode keys only** (`rzp_test_*`) while the kit is alpha. Live keys are refused by the deployment guide.</Callout>

## Overview

1. Create a Razorpay test account and test keys (steps here are **untested**).
2. Put the keys in site_config (`saas_core_razorpay_key_id`, `..._key_secret`, `..._webhook_secret`), run the `bootstrap_gateway` op, then blank the secrets.
3. Point the Razorpay webhook at `https://<site>/api/method/saas_core.webhooks.razorpay`. A local site needs a public tunnel (**untested**).
4. Pay with Razorpay's published test card or UPI.

Billing is monthly in advance; cancel refunds unused whole days pro rata; upgrades are charged by payment link. Receipts only, no tax invoices.
