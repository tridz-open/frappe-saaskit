# Used by

<p className="lede">Products and teams building with Frappe SaaS.</p>

> [!NOTE]
> Frappe SaaS is alpha. Nothing here is a claim of production use. URY currently runs on a beta of the kit.

## URY

[URY](https://ury.app) is an open-source restaurant POS and ERP built on Frappe. It is built with the kit for signup, billing and Frappe Cloud provisioning, and currently runs on a beta.

<Screenshot name="ury-console-instances" alt="URY customer console listing two example workspaces with status and URL" caption="The URY customer console, built from the starter (example data)." />

## Tridz

[Tridz](https://tridz.com) is the software company behind the kit and the Frappe products built on it.

## Huf

[Huf](https://huf.ai) is an open-source multi-agent AI framework built on Frappe.

## Franchise Media

[Franchise Media](https://www.thefranchisemedia.com/) is a franchise consulting and media company.

More Tridz products will be listed here as they adopt it.
