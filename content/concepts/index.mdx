# Concepts

<p className="lede">Eight ideas explain the whole kit.</p>

<Cards num={2}>
  <Cards.Card title="Tenants" href="/concepts/tenants/" icon={<Icon name="Server" />}>A tenant is one customer's isolated Frappe site.</Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Plans and prices" href="/concepts/plans/" icon={<Icon name="Wallet" />}>What a customer buys, per interval and currency.</Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Subscriptions" href="/concepts/subscriptions/" icon={<Icon name="RefreshCw" />}>Ties a tenant to a plan and a gateway.</Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Provisioning" href="/concepts/provisioning/" icon={<Icon name="Cloud" />}>Turns a paid tenant into a running site, resume-safe.</Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Golden backup" href="/concepts/golden-backup/" icon={<Icon name="Layers" />}>A prepared site you snapshot once and clone per customer.</Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Templates and recipes" href="/concepts/recipes/" icon={<Icon name="Puzzle" />}>What a site is, plus optional code to customise it.</Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Payment gateways" href="/concepts/gateways/" icon={<Icon name="Plug" />}>A pluggable class; billing never imports a provider.</Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Approvals" href="/concepts/approvals/" icon={<Icon name="ShieldCheck" />}>Destructive operations wait for a second person.</Cards.Card>
</Cards>

A **Plan** points at a **Template**; checkout creates a **Tenant** and a **Subscription**; a paid event starts a **Provision Job** that restores the **golden backup** and runs the **recipe** hooks; destructive jobs wait for an **Approval**.
